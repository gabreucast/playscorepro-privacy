Privacy Policy

PlayScorePro

Last updated: October 2026

PlayScorePro is a scorekeeping application designed to help users track points and victories during card game matches, including games such as Truco.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use PlayScorePro.

1. Information Collection and Use

PlayScorePro does not require account creation and does not directly ask users to provide personal information such as email addresses, phone numbers, contacts, photos, or location data.

PlayScorePro uses Firebase Crashlytics, a service provided by Google, to help identify, analyze, and fix technical problems. Crashlytics may automatically collect diagnostic information, including crash reports, stack traces, app state, device information, operating system version, app version, and identifiers used by the crash reporting service.

2. Local Data Storage

PlayScorePro may store game-related information on your device, such as player names, scores, and match history, to provide the application's functionality.

This game-related information is not intentionally transmitted to Firebase Crashlytics as part of its standard crash reporting functionality.

3. Advertising

PlayScorePro does not currently display advertisements or use advertising services such as Google AdMob.

If advertising services are introduced in a future version, this Privacy Policy will be updated to describe the relevant data practices.

4. Third-Party Services

PlayScorePro uses Firebase Crashlytics for crash reporting and diagnostics. This service is provided by Google and may process diagnostic information as described in this policy.

For more information, please consult:

  • Firebase Privacy and Security
  • Firebase Crashlytics Documentation
  • Google Privacy Policy

5. Data Sharing

PlayScorePro does not sell users' personal information. Diagnostic information may be processed by Firebase Crashlytics to provide crash reporting and help improve application stability.

6. Children's Privacy

PlayScorePro does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. The application uses a third-party crash reporting service as described in this policy.

7. Data Security

PlayScorePro stores game-related information locally on the user's device. Firebase Crashlytics processes diagnostic information to help identify and resolve technical issues.

No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. Users should keep their devices protected.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Game-related information stored locally can generally be removed by clearing the application's data or uninstalling the application, although device backups may affect whether data can be restored.

Diagnostic data collected by Firebase Crashlytics is handled according to Google's applicable retention practices and policies.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application or its data practices change. Updates will be published on this page with a revised date.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the application's privacy practices, contact:

Email: castgabreu@gmail.com
