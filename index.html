Privacy Policy – PlayScorePro

Last updated: September 2026

PlayScorePro is a scorekeeping application designed to help users track points and victories during card game matches such as Truco.

Information Collection and Use

PlayScorePro does not require account creation and does not directly request personal information such as email addresses, phone numbers, contacts, photos, microphone access, or location data.

Game-related information, such as player names, scores, victories, and match history, may be stored locally on the user's device to provide the application's functionality.

Local Data Storage

PlayScorePro stores game-related information locally on the user's device, such as player names, scores, victories, and match history.

This game-related information is used solely to provide the application's functionality and is not transmitted to external servers by PlayScorePro.

Crash Reporting

PlayScorePro uses Firebase Crashlytics, a crash reporting service provided by Google, to help identify, diagnose, and fix application crashes and improve application stability.

Firebase Crashlytics may automatically collect information associated with application crashes, including crash stack traces, relevant application state, device information, application version information, operating system information, and identifiers used by Crashlytics to associate and measure crash reports.

Crashlytics data is processed by Google Firebase in accordance with Google's privacy and data processing practices.

Advertising

At the time of this publication, PlayScorePro does not display advertisements and does not use advertising services.

Future versions of the application may integrate advertising services. If advertising services are implemented, this Privacy Policy and the application's Google Play Data Safety information will be updated accordingly.

Third-Party Services

PlayScorePro currently uses Firebase Crashlytics for crash reporting.

Third-party services may process technical information according to their respective privacy policies and terms.

Children's Privacy

PlayScorePro does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users through account registration or direct requests for personal information.

Crash reporting information may be automatically processed by Firebase Crashlytics as described in the Crash Reporting section.

Security

PlayScorePro does not directly collect or transmit personal information through its core gameplay functionality.

Technical information processed through Firebase Crashlytics is handled by Google according to Firebase's security and privacy practices.

Data Retention and Deletion

Game-related information stored locally by PlayScorePro remains on the user's device and can be removed by clearing the application's data or uninstalling the application, subject to the operating system's behavior.

Crash reporting data is processed and retained by Firebase Crashlytics according to Google's applicable data retention practices.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to the application, its services, or applicable requirements. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
