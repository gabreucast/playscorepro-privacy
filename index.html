# Privacy Policy – PlayScorePro Last updated: June 2026 PlayScorePro is a scorekeeping application designed to help users track points and victories during card game matches such as Truco. ## Information Collection and Use PlayScorePro does not collect, store, transmit, or share personal information directly. The application does not require account creation and does not request personal information such as email addresses, phone numbers, contacts, photos, microphone access, or location data. ## Local Data Storage PlayScorePro may store game-related information locally on the user's device, such as player names, scores, and match history, solely to provide the application's functionality. This information remains on the user's device and is not transmitted to external servers. ## Advertising At the time of this publication, PlayScorePro does not display advertisements. Future versions of the application may integrate advertising services such as Google AdMob. If advertising services are implemented, they may collect certain information automatically, including advertising identifiers and usage-related data, in accordance with their own privacy policies. For more information about how Google handles data, please visit: https://policies.google.com/privacy Any future integration of advertising services will be reflected in updates to this Privacy Policy and in the application's Google Play Data Safety section. ## Third-Party Services PlayScorePro may use third-party services in future versions, including analytics, crash reporting, or advertising services. Such services will process data according to their respective privacy policies. ## Children's Privacy PlayScorePro does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users. ## Security Since PlayScorePro does not collect personal data directly, no personal information is transmitted to or stored on external servers by the application. ## Changes to This Privacy Policy This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. ## Contact If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact: Castillo Devs Email: [castgabreu@gmail.com](mailto:castgabreu@gmail.com)
